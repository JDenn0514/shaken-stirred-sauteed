Prototype  throwaway · not site code

One cook, several recipes, in order

The question: if the planner fills each dish's waits with another dish's hands-on steps, and keeps clashing dishes out of the oven, does it pick an order you'd actually follow? You never see a clock, only the next step and any timers. The plan re-orders itself when a step runs long.

The recipes are the site's real ones, with the new planning data filled in by hand: how long your hands are busy, what runs on its own, what needs the oven, broiler, a burner or the microwave, and how long a dish can sit once it's done. Ranges plan from the middle.
