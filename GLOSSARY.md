# Shaken, Stirred & Sautéed

A phone-first recipe site: Jacob's recipes for everyone to read and cook, and, for invited friends and family, a private cookbook of their own.

## People

**Visitor**:
Someone using the site without signing in.
_Avoid_: guest, anonymous user

**Owner**:
Jacob, the one person who signs in with GitHub, changes the public recipes and invites members.
_Avoid_: editor, admin

**Member**:
A signed-in person other than the owner, who has a cookbook.
_Avoid_: user, signed-in person, friend

## Recipes

**Public recipes**:
The owner's recipes, published for everyone to read and cook.
_Avoid_: shared recipes, the library

**Cookbook**:
A member's own recipes, seen only by that member.
_Avoid_: account, collection, my recipes

**Copy**:
A recipe in a member's cookbook made from a public recipe with "Add to my cookbook", and from then on independent of it.
_Avoid_: fork, saved recipe, bookmark

## Access

**Invite list**:
The email addresses allowed to become members by signing in with Google.
_Avoid_: allowlist, waitlist
