A brand direction for Shaken, Stirred & Sautéed

Tin-Glaze

Blue-and-white tin-glazed tile: the shared language of kitchen walls from Lisbon to Palermo to Jerusalem to Delft. Every recipe is a tile set in grout, and where four tiles meet, a cobalt rosette appears.

See the wall → Painkiller Lemon Blueberry Tart Risotto Milanese
01

The story

The site is a tiled backsplash. Recipes are tiles. The tiles are calm in the middle, so you can read them, and ornamental at the joints.

Why tile

Tiled walls are where all three verbs happen: at the stove, at the sink and at the home bar. The cooking here runs along the Mediterranean and the Levant, with Israeli salad, chicken shawarma, tahini sauces, Italian wedding soup, risotto Milanese and chimichurri, plus a lot of lemon. Tin-glazed tile is the one decorative tradition all of those kitchens share.

Why only cobalt

Food sites tend to go warm, with cream, terracotta and rust. Tin-Glaze turns cool instead: one cobalt hue fired on glossy white, with deep cobalt for ink. Categories are told apart by their pattern before their color, so the page stays quiet and food photographs supply the warmth.

Why lemon, once

A single Sicilian-lemon accent is kept for the moments that matter while you cook: the step you are on and the ingredients you have used. It also paints the ampersand in the name. If lemon shows up anywhere else it stops meaning “you are here”.

  • Calm centre, busy joints. Ornament lives in the corners and edges and never sits behind text.
  • One hand, wet fingers. Every target is at least 42–44px. State is shown by fill and weight, never by color alone.
  • Modular to the bone. Tiles, grout, frieze and medallion are four parts that build every screen.

02

Logo

The mark is a single square tile with a four-fold cobalt motif built from the three tools. A shaker seen from above sits at the centre with a lemon cap. Four stirring spoons form the cross arms, and pan handles reach into the corners, where they become quarter-rosettes. The wordmark is set in Young Serif and converted to outlines, and its ampersand is painted lemon with a cobalt edge.

Clear space

Leave at least a quarter of the mark’s height (one corner-rosette radius) clear on every side. At 40px in the header that is 10px.

Minimum size

The mark alone can go down to 24px, where the spoons still read. The full lockup needs at least 180px of width. Below that, use the mark and set the name in live text.

  • Do use cobalt on glaze white or on a tile face.
  • Do switch to the night lockup on cobalt grounds.
  • Don’t recolor it. Terracotta and warm browns belong to someone else’s kitchen.
  • Don’t stretch, squash or rotate the lockup. Only the mark may turn, and only in 90° steps.
  • Don’t set it over pattern. Ornament never runs behind the name.
  • 🍸Shaken, Stirred & Sautéed
    Don’t go back to an emoji or a stock icon for the mark.

09

Photography & illustration

  • Palette in frame: whites, cobalt ceramics, steel, linen and lemon. The food supplies the warmth.
  • Inset, never full-bleed: on a tile the photo sits inside a 26px glaze margin with a 1.5px cobalt keyline (already in .card-img and .recipe-hero), like a painted panel set into the wall.
  • Crops: 16:10 for tiles and 16:9 for recipe heroes. Keep the subject inside the middle 70%, because the corner quarters sit over the frame.
  • Illustration: if photos are missing, use a single motif in cobalt line on the tile face. Never use clip-art food or emoji.
  • Avoid: warm filters, dark moody wood, heavy vignettes, and terracotta-and-rust styling.
10

How to adopt

  1. Copy the theme: style-guides/tin-glaze/theme.css → assets/style.css (replace it).
  2. Copy the fonts: style-guides/tin-glaze/fonts/ → assets/fonts/ (both .woff2 files). The @font-face rules point at ./fonts/, which resolves next to the stylesheet.
  3. Copy the icon: style-guides/tin-glaze/icon.svg → assets/icon.svg. Optionally regenerate icon-192.png and icon-512.png from mark.svg.
  4. Swap the header brand in index.html and recipe.html: replace the 🍸 <span class="brand-mark"> with the inline <svg class="brand-mark"> from preview/index.html. It uses classes only, with no style="", so it stays CSP-safe and follows dark mode.
  5. Theme color: replace <meta name="theme-color" content="#b4532a"> with two metas, #F7F9FC for (prefers-color-scheme: light) and #0A1638 for dark. Set theme_color/background_color in manifest.webmanifest to #F7F9FC.
  6. Optional copy pass: take the voice lines from section 08 into home.js and recipe.js.
