01Concept

A risograph prints one colour per drum. Two drums give you two inks, and where one lands on the other you get a third colour for free. Riso prints are also never quite in register, so the plates drift by a hair. That is where this theme comes from.

Round one turned all of that up to full volume. This round treats it the way a good small-press cookbook would: generous paper, clean type, and the printing showing through in a few deliberate places. The recipe is the thing you read; the brand is the thing you notice after.

The owner cooks from a phone. So the rules below favour reading at arm's length with wet hands: calm body text, clear states, big targets and nothing that moves unless you touched it.

  1. Two inks, three colours. Pink is cooking, blue is cocktails, and baking gets the purple where they overlap. No other hues.
  2. One shake, two places. The out-of-register print appears only on the logo ampersand and the recipe title. Every other heading is set clean.
  3. Yellow means now. The highlighter marks the step you are on. It is never used for decoration.
  4. One elevation. Cards and the dropdown sit on a 3px pink plate, slightly off. No blur shadows, no rotation, no halftone floods.

03Logo

The wordmark is the name set in Bricolage Grotesque Bold. Its ampersand is printed twice, a blue plate and a pink plate slightly out of register, with purple where they overlap. On its own, that shaken ampersand is the mark: favicon, home-screen icon and stamp.

Usage

  • Clear space on all sides equals the height of the ampersand's lower bowl (about a third of its height).
  • The plate offset is fixed: pink sits up and to the right of blue by about 6% of the glyph. Never animate it or exaggerate it.
  • The rest of the wordmark is always one solid ink (blue-black on paper, near-white on dark).
  • In the site header the name is live text, so it stays crisp and searchable; only the ampersand is an inline SVG.

Don't

  • Shake any other letter, or the whole wordmark.
  • Recolour the plates (no gold, no yellow, no gradients).
  • Put the mark on a halftone, a photo or a busy pattern.
  • Rotate it, outline it or add a drop shadow.
  • Bring back the emoji as a logo.

08Voice and microcopy

Written like the margin notes in a well-used cookbook: plain, warm, specific. Say what to do and how you'll know it's done. Short sentences, sentence case, no exclamation marks, no puns in the interface.

  • Lead with the verb. "Fold in the berries," not "The berries should be folded in."
  • Give the cue, not just the clock. "Until it smells nutty" beats "3 minutes."
  • Grams first, cups second. Both always, separated by a middle dot.
  • Labels are nouns. Makes, Total, Glass, Ice. No colons.

09Photography

The site works without photos, and that is the default. When a recipe does get one, it should look like it belongs in a small printed cookbook.

  • Daylight, from the side. A window, not a flash. Soft shadows are fine.
  • Shot from above or at 45°, on a plain, light surface: a board, a tea towel, paper.
  • Real food, a bit messy. Crumbs, a drip, a used spoon. Not styled to perfection.
  • Full colour, no filters. The theme frames photos with a blue outline and the pink plate, so the photo itself needs no treatment.
  • 16:10 on cards, 16:9 on the recipe. Keep the food in the middle third so either crop works.

10How to adopt

  1. Copy style-guides/overprint-quiet/theme.css to assets/style.css, and the fonts/ folder to assets/fonts/. The @font-face rule uses ./fonts/, so it resolves relative to the stylesheet.
  2. Copy logo.svg to assets/icon.svg. Regenerate icon-192.png and icon-512.png from it, centred on #F6F4EE with about 18% padding.
  3. In index.html and recipe.html, replace the <a class="brand"> contents with the header from preview/index.html (live text, the inline SVG ampersand, and a visually hidden "&"). Without that change the theme still works: it hides the emoji and prints a text ampersand in two plates.
  4. Replace theme-color with the two media-scoped tags from the previews (#F6F4EE light, #16141B dark), and set theme_color and background_color in manifest.webmanifest to #F6F4EE.
  5. Nothing to change in sw.js: it is network-first and caches the font on the first visit, so the type works offline after that.
  6. No JavaScript changes. The CSP stays as it is: no inline styles, no external hosts.
