The site works like a restaurant's expediting system. The home page is the rail of open tickets. A recipe is one ticket, read top to bottom while you fire it. Filters print up from the bottom like paper feeding out of a thermal printer. At night the paper rail turns into the glowing kitchen display screen used during service.

01

Story

Why a ticket and not a cookbook.

Shaken, stirred, sautéed. Those are calls made at a pass, and the bar and the line both run on tickets.

The owner writes recipes the way a line cook writes specs: grams and millilitres side by side, 3/4 oz scaled to land on a standard jigger line, and a wait time and a cue on each step. That is already ticket language, so the brand leans into it.

There are no photos yet, and a print-and-type direction loses nothing without them. Every ornament here comes from the real object: torn thermal paper, a chrome rail, a barcode, dotted price leaders, a highlighter swipe, a grease-pencil strike. Even the print stylesheet has a reason to exist, because the site prints as an actual 80 mm ticket.

The rail · home

Open tickets

Cards hang from a chrome rail, one rail per row, at their natural length like real tickets. Each has a colored order-pad flag for its station.

The ticket · recipe

Read it, fire it

Facts print as a receipt. Steps are numbered 01, 02. The step you're on gets a highlighter and a FIRING tag. Finished steps get a SENT stamp.

The screen · dark mode

Night service

In dark mode the paper becomes a kitchen display screen: black glass, colored ticket headers, phosphor green for live states, amber timers.

02

Logo

A ticket header set in double-height mono, closed by a Code 39 barcode whose bars really encode *SSS*. All lettering is outlined from Martian Mono at wdth 75 / wght 800, so the SVGs need no font.

Do & 86 it

Upright, on steel, paper or ink. Let the rail stay level.

Don't tilt the rail. Tickets swing; the rail never does.

Don't stretch it or recolor the rush stripe.

🍸

No emoji as the logo. The cocktail glass is retired.

Type

One family, one file. Martian Mono variable (wdth 75–112.5, wght 100–800), SIL Open Font License 1.1, self-hosted as fonts/martian-mono-latin-standard-normal.woff2 (38 KB). Width does the job other brands give to a second family.

Rules

Double-height for names. Brand, h1 and card titles are wdth 75, wght 800, uppercase, line-height 0.95, and they may wrap.

Narrow for reading. Body and steps are wdth 87.5, 16 px minimum, line-height 1.5 to 1.55, sentence case. Mono needs the narrower width to keep a line readable on a 375 px phone.

Wide for labels. dt, sheet groups and counts are wdth 100, wght 500, 11 to 12 px, uppercase, tracking 0.08em.

Tabular figures everywhere. Grams and ounces line up.

Voice

Terse, shouted line calls, warm underneath. Jargon is always a kicker beside the plain words, never a replacement, so a screen reader and a first-time guest both get "Done", "Clear all" and "Keep screen on".

Call it short

Two or three words, uppercase, no exclamation marks. The kitchen is loud; the type does the shouting.

Sentence case to cook by

Steps, notes and ledes stay in calm sentence case. Only names and calls go to caps.

Warm underneath

"Nothing on the rail" is a shrug, not an error. Never blame the cook.

09

Imagery

When photos arrive, they print. The theme already renders .card-img and .recipe-hero as thermal halftone: grayscale, contrast 1.35, multiplied into the paper (a green phosphor tint on the KDS).

Illustration direction · halftone dot, one spot color
  • Shot at the passOverhead or dead-level, on steel or a sheet pan, with the plate centered like it's about to be run. No props, no linen, no hands.
  • Printed, not posedEvery image runs through the thermal treatment, so phone photos and pro photos look like the same printer made them.
  • One spot colorIllustrations are halftone dots in ink, with at most one flag color or the highlighter. Never a gradient, never a drop shadow.
  • One plate, one ticketA recipe gets one hero image, 16:9, above the title on the ticket. Cards use 16:10 crops of the same file.

How to adopt

No build step and no JavaScript changes. Three file copies and one header edit in two HTML files.

  1. Copy style-guides/ticket-rail/theme.css over assets/style.css.
  2. Copy style-guides/ticket-rail/fonts/ to assets/fonts/. theme.css loads ./fonts/martian-mono-latin-standard-normal.woff2 relative to itself.
  3. Copy logo.svg to assets/icon.svg (the favicon). Re-export icon-192.png and icon-512.png from it.
  4. In index.html and recipe.html, replace the 🍸 brand-mark span with the inline SVG and update theme-color (right).
  5. In manifest.webmanifest, set theme_color to #141414 and background_color to #CDD1D3. Add assets/fonts/…woff2 to the service worker's cache list if you want the font offline.
