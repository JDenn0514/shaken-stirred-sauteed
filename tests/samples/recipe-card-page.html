Sesame Noodles

Ingredients
  • 200g/7oz egg noodles (note 1)
  • 1 tbsp oil
Sesame Sauce (Note 2)
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp sugar (or honey)
Instructions

  1. Mix the Sesame Sauce ingredients in a bowl.

  2. Boil the noodles for 3 minutes, drain, then toss with the oil and the sauce.

Recipe Notes

1. Any thin wheat noodles work. Ramen noodles are fine.

Don't use rice noodles.

2. Adjust the sauce to your liking.
