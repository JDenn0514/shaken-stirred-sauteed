Quick Pickled Onions

Makes 1 jar

Ingredients

  • 1 red onion, thinly sliced
  • 1 cup cider vinegar
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons kosher salt

Instructions

  1. Pack the onion into a jar.
  2. Stir the vinegar, sugar and salt until dissolved, and pour over the onion.
  3. Leave at room temperature for 1 hour, then keep in the fridge.
